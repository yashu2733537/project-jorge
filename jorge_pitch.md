# jorge — the AI that runs your business while you sleep

> Your personal AI sales & ops assistant that actually DOES things — not just chats.

## The pitch

Most "AI assistants" just talk. jorge **acts**. It lives on your machine, works with real
accounts (email, Instagram, WhatsApp, browsers), and gets real work done end-to-end —
drafting, sending, researching, uploading, organizing, even delegating coding work.

## What jorge does

| Capability | What you get |
|---|---|
| 📧 Email automation | Drafts + sends tone-aware emails with AI, attaches files, remembers contacts |
| 🔍 Research | Deep web research with cited sources, price checks, comparisons |
| 🖥️ Real browser | Logs into your accounts, fills forms, scrapes, uploads (captcha/2FA safe) |
| 📤 Instagram | Uploads reels/videos to Instagram on command |
| 💬 Chat brain | Natural conversation that picks the right tool automatically |
| 🗣️ Voice + GUI | Desktop avatar that listens (mic), talks back (TTS), and chats in a clean window |
| 📂 Auto-organize | Sorts Downloads/Desktop by type and naming patterns |
| ⚙️ Task delegation | Breaks big jobs into steps and hands them to a senior coding agent |
| 🔔 Monitoring | Watches CPU/memory/disk and alerts on problems |
